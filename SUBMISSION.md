Budgeting apps show you a pie chart and you feel nothing. Receipt Court prosecutes the purchase instead. You file a charge — merchant, amount, time — and a deterministic engine scores it 0–100 against the whole docket. The prosecution indicts. You type a plea, or appoint a public defender who types a mediocre one that the court is built to demolish. That plea is cross-examined: “it was on sale” and “I deserved it” make things worse. The court then stamps GUILTY, GUILTY WITH LENIENCY, NOT GUILTY, or CASE DISMISSED — a full-screen slam, a red stamp, a synthesised gavel. An optional live DA argues from the same exhibits. Being forced to defend a 2:41am DoorDash order is the intervention. A bar chart is not.
