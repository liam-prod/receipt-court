Budgeting apps show you a pie chart and you feel nothing. Receipt Court prosecutes the purchase instead. You file a charge — merchant, amount, time — and a deterministic engine scores it 0–100 against the whole docket, entering exhibits into evidence. The prosecution delivers an indictment. You type a plea in your own words. That plea is cross-examined: “it was on sale” and “I deserved it” make things worse; “it was for work” or “it broke” help. The court then stamps GUILTY, GUILTY WITH LENIENCY, NOT GUILTY, or CASE DISMISSED, with a sentence, a red stamp slam, and a synthesised gavel bang. Being forced to defend a 2:41am DoorDash order is the intervention. A bar chart is not.
